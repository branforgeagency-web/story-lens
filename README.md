# STORY LENS — Creative Production Studio

> *"Translating brand moments into visceral perspectives. Flexing between the quietude of the ethereal and the grit of the expressive."*

A bespoke duplicate of the **[Velour Productions](https://velourproductions.com/)** homepage, engineered for **Story Lens** with luxury obsidian black (`#050505`), champagne gold (`#D4AF37`, `#E5C158`), and optical white (`#FFFFFF`) accents.

Specializing in **Commercial Videography**, **9:16 Ad Reels**, **140km/h FPV Drone Cinematography**, and **Studio Soundstage Productions**.
